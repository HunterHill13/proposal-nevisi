# Proposal-Nevisi Historical Evolution & Architectural Changelog

This document preserves the comprehensive architectural milestones, version history, and historical test suites of the **Proposal-Nevisi Engine**.

---

## Complete Version History

### Version 14.1.0 (Current Release)
- **Full English Directive Standard:** All agent manifests, operational guidelines, and core skill directives rewritten in 100% professional English for superior model instruction following.
- **Biostatistical Verification Hardening (`verify_biostatistics.py`):** Replaced hardcoded sample size constants with dynamic parametric calculations across 6 study archetypes. Unknown or unsupported study families strictly return `UNVERIFIED` with diagnostic guidance.
- **Native Recursive OMML Builder:** Built-in recursive fraction (`<m:f>`) and radical (`<m:rad>`) OMML parser in `NativeOmmlMathEngine`, guaranteeing zero raw text leakage even without external conversion libraries.
- **Clean Author Manuscript Delivery Standard:** Reframed formatting rules to ensure grant proposals delivered to university review portals are clean, authoritative research protocols without assistant meta-commentary.
- **Strict Schema Enforcement:** Hardened all 10 JSON schemas in `schemas/` with `additionalProperties: false`.
- **Package Hygiene:** Purged legacy binaries, temporary test outputs, and unused fixtures, achieving a lightweight, high-performance release package.

### Version 14.0.0
- **Cognitive Study Section Swarm:** Introduced a 6-persona study section (Significance, Methodology, Evidence, Feasibility, Ethics, Academic Voice) plus SRO Synthesizer.
- **Two-Tier Nested Closed-Loop:** Automated multi-round remediation (up to 10 Study Section rounds and up to 5 Post-Draft Return Loops).
- **Academic Style & Voice Lens:** Integrated Academic Humanizer 6-layer principles into peer review.

### Version 13.2.0
- **Dual-Repo Skills Synchronization:** Integrated `scripts/sync_skills.py` for atomic multi-repo synchronization and global Antigravity mirroring.
- **Native Word OMML Equations:** Replaced raw LaTeX and Word EQ fields with native Office Math Markup Language.

### Version 13.0.0
- **EGM (Elephant-Goldfish Model):** Architectural decoupling between the deep-context writing Elephant and zero-context isolated Goldfish reviewers.
- **Litmaps & Inciteful Network Graphing:** True multi-hop citation snowballing and biomedical concept mapping.

### Version 12.1.0
- **Six-Archetype Polymorphism:** Explicit support for Health Systems, Clinical RCTs, In Vivo Animal Models, In Vitro Cell Culture, Diagnostic Accuracy, and Usability/Digital Health.
